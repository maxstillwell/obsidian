Hi Max,

Apologies for the delay in getting this across to you. We have undertaken an initial review of the parcels and provided an overview of the relevant controls and dwelling entitlements below.

All of the parcels are located within the Farming Zone (FZ). The purpose of the FZ is:

- To implement the Municipal Planning Strategy and the Planning Policy Framework.
- To provide for the use of land for agriculture. To encourage the retention of productive agricultural land.
- To ensure that non-agricultural uses, including dwellings, do not adversely affect the use of land for agriculture.
- To encourage the retention of employment and population to support rural communities.
- To encourage use and development of land based on comprehensive and sustainable land management practices and infrastructure provision.
- To provide for the use and development of land for the specific purposes identified in a schedule to this zone.

Any application for use and/or development will need to respond to the above stated purpose.

Under the FZ, the following requirements apply:

- Minimum subdivision area – each lot created must be a minimum of 40ha
- Minimum area for which no permit is required to use the land as a dwelling – 40ha
- Minimum setback from a road

- 100m from a road in a Transport 2 Zone
- 20m from any other road

- Minimum setback from a boundary – 5m
- Minimum setback from a dwelling not in the same ownership – 100m

We note from a review of the aerial imagery, there does not appear to be any existing dwellings on any of the lots however we note there is significant vegetation across a large portion of the site. Consideration will need to be given to the value of this vegetation and whether any of it is considered to be native vegetation as this may influence your ability to clear vegetation on the land.

In addition, we note the sites are not affected by any flooding control overlays such as a Special Building Overlay, Flood Overlay or Land Subject to Inundation Overlay.

A number of the sites are affected by the Bushfire Management Overlay, pursuant to which a bushfire hazard assessment, a bushfire landscape assessment and a bushfire management statement would need to be prepared as part of any future development application. Consideration will also need to be given to construction standards and access arrangements as well as the provision of defendable space and water supply.

In addition to the above, Clause 35.07-2 of the Farming Zone sets out the following mandatory requirements for dwellings.

- A lot used for a dwelling, small second dwelling or rural worker accommodation must meet the following requirements:

- Access to the dwelling, small second dwelling or rural worker accommodation must be provided via an all-weather road with dimensions adequate to accommodate emergency vehicles.
- Each dwelling, small second dwelling or rural worker accommodation must be connected to reticulated sewerage, if available. If reticulated sewerage is not available all wastewater from each dwelling must be treated and retained within the lot in accordance with the requirements of the Environment Protection Regulations under the Environment Protection Act 2017 for an on-site wastewater management system.
- The dwelling, small second dwelling or rural worker accommodation must be connected to a reticulated potable water supply or have an alternative potable water supply with adequate storage for domestic use as well as for fire fighting purposes.
- The dwelling, small second dwelling or rural worker accommodation must be connected to a reticulated electricity supply or have an alternative energy source.
