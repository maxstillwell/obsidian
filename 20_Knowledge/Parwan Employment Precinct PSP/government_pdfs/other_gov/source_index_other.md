## Current-status synthesis (official sources only)

- **No gazetted PEP PSP or precinct-specific UGZ schedule was identified** in the current Moorabool Planning Scheme by 19 August 2026. PEP remains a state/local strategic employment project rather than a demonstrated completed statutory PSP.
- The current state program identifies **1,500 potential jobs**, **no homes**, and **Wurundjeri Woi-Wurrung** Traditional Owners. This is a planning-program capacity figure, not development approval or committed delivery.
- Statutory policy expects a mix of agribusiness and industrial use, with key interfaces to the Maddingley brown-coal resource/mine, gas transmission pipeline, nearby industry and sensitive uses. It also supports the **Eastern Link Road**, rail/highway-related access, potable and recycled water, and stormwater controls to protect the Werribee River and tributaries.
- WIN is the most material official water/environment project found in the PEP vicinity. Its referral decision was **no EES required subject to conditions**, while PBID Stage 1 has a separate Commonwealth controlled-action process. It is supporting infrastructure/context, not evidence that PEP itself is approved or serviced.
