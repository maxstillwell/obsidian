## Notes on planning status and maps

- No gazetted Parwan Employment PSP, DCP, planning-scheme amendment package or final precinct map was located on the official VPA project page as at the research date. This is consistent with the project’s “in progress” consultation status.
- The authoritative current boundary/location description is on the VPA project page: approximately 2,500 ha south of Bacchus Marsh; bounded by Parwan Creek, Nortons Road/wastewater plant, Nerowie and Glenmore Roads, and Bacchus Marsh–Balliang Road; bisected by Geelong–Bacchus Marsh Road. Technical-report figures provide the available official working maps.
- `10_gas_safety_2023.pdf` and `15_safety_management_2023.pdf` have matching study title/date/content but distinct VPA-hosted URLs; preserve both until a later document-control review confirms whether the 2026-hosted link is a republished copy.
