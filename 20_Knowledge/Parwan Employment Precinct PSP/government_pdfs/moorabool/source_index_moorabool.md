# Parwan Employment Precinct PSP — Moorabool / Victorian Government source index

Research cut-off: 19 August 2026.  Official government sources only.  Dates are publication/document dates where stated; otherwise the relevant official page's last-update/status date is used.

## Status as at the research cut-off

There is **no approved Parwan Employment PSP or dedicated exhibited planning-scheme amendment** available in the official sources reviewed.  The VPA project list (last updated 24 April 2026) records it as **In Progress — Council & State Agency Consultation**.  The most recent project update (16 February 2026) says technical work is informing a draft place-based plan and PSP documentation, before Council and State-agency validation.  This follows recommencement after a 2023 pause associated with Victorian Grassland Earless Dragon habitat assessment.
