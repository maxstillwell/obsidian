## 5. Membership Structure

The Committee resolved to establish the following membership category:

### Full Membership

Annual Membership Fee:

**AUD $200 per annum**

Benefits may include:

- Access to organised hunting activities on private rural properties.
    
- Participation in camping, fishing and outdoor recreation events.
    
- Access to member communications and newsletters.
    
- Invitations to educational and fieldcraft activities.
    
- Access to selected member-only events and facilities.
    

All applications shall be subject to Committee approval.

Moved: Cong Wang

Seconded: Xiaogang Wu

Carried unanimously.

## 6. Membership Application Process

The Committee approved the implementation of an online membership application process through the Association website.

Applications shall include:

- Personal details
    
- Emergency contact information
    
- Outdoor interests
    
- Agreement to the Association Code of Conduct
    
- Acknowledgement of outdoor activity risks
    

Membership shall not become active until:

1. Committee approval is granted.
    
2. Membership fees are paid.
