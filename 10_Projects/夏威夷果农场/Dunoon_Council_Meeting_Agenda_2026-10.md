# Dunoon / Dirahny Ecovillage  
## Meeting Agenda – Lismore City Council

**Meeting purpose:**  
To clarify the current strategic planning status, Council’s intended programme for the Dunoon Master Plan, the role of major landowners during that process, Council’s preliminary view of the Dirahny Ecovillage submission, and the statutory pathway from the Master Plan through to rezoning and future development approvals.

**Project background:**  
- Property: **1306 Dunoon Road, Dunoon**
- Total landholding: **142.49 ha**
- Area identified within the Dunoon Village Growth Area: **101.69 ha**
- The landowner lodged the **Dirahny Ecovillage submission in June 2026** as part of the LSPS / Rural Land Use Strategy process.
- Lismore City Council adopted the new Strategic Planning Framework in September 2026.
- The landowner intends to remain actively engaged with Council through the next stage of strategic and statutory planning.

---

## 1. NSW Government Endorsement – Current Status and Timing

We would appreciate an update on the status of the Strategic Planning Framework following Council adoption.

**Discussion points**
- Has the adopted framework now been formally submitted to NSW DPHI for endorsement?
- What are the remaining steps before State endorsement is completed?
- Does Council currently have an indicative timeframe for endorsement?
- Is a **3–6 month timeframe** considered realistic based on Council’s current discussions with DPHI?
- Are any further studies, amendments or information requests expected before endorsement?
- Is there a nominated Council or DPHI officer responsible for this part of the process?

**Requested outcome:**  
A clear understanding of the current endorsement status, remaining steps and Council’s best available working timeframe.

---

## 2. Dunoon Master Plan – Status, Priority and Work Programme

The timing of the Dunoon Master Plan is one of the key matters we would like to clarify.

**Discussion points**
- Has work on the Dunoon Master Plan already commenced?
- Is the Master Plan included in Council’s current **2026/27 or 2027/28 work programme and budget**?
- Will the Master Plan be prepared internally by Council or through external consultants?
- What is Council’s current anticipated programme for:
  - commencement;
  - technical investigations;
  - landowner consultation;
  - preliminary concept planning;
  - draft Master Plan;
  - public exhibition; and
  - final adoption?
- Is an approximately **12-month Master Plan preparation period** considered realistic?
- What matters are likely to determine or delay the programme?

**Requested outcome:**  
Council’s current working programme for the Dunoon Master Plan, including expected commencement and target milestones.

---

## 3. Dunoon’s Priority Within the Lismore Growth Strategy

We would like to better understand how Council currently views Dunoon in the broader sequencing of future housing growth across the Lismore LGA.

**Discussion points**
- How does Council currently rank or sequence Dunoon relative to the other identified growth areas?
- Does Council regard Dunoon as a **short-, medium- or longer-term housing growth area**?
- What factors led Council to identify Dunoon for approximately **362 ha of future residential growth and an indicative 434 dwellings**?
- Based on Council’s current research and infrastructure planning, when does Council expect Dunoon to begin making a material contribution to housing supply?
- Are there any strategic dependencies that must occur before Dunoon can progress?

**Requested outcome:**  
A clearer understanding of the role and timing Council expects Dunoon to play in Lismore’s broader housing strategy.

---

## 4. Landowner Participation During the Master Plan

Our property represents a significant portion of the identified Dunoon Village Growth Area, and we would like to engage constructively throughout the process.

**Discussion points**
- How does Council intend to engage major landowners during preparation of the Master Plan?
- Will Council establish a landowner working group, project reference group or regular stakeholder meetings?
- Can major landowners receive progress updates as technical work and concept planning develops?
- At what stages will landowners be able to provide:
  - site information;
  - servicing investigations;
  - environmental studies;
  - concept plans; and
  - feedback on draft layouts?
- Can Council nominate a primary project contact for ongoing communication?

**Requested outcome:**  
Agreement on a practical mechanism for ongoing landowner participation and regular project updates.

---

## 5. June 2026 Dirahny Ecovillage Submission – Council Feedback

The landowner lodged the Dirahny Ecovillage submission in June 2026. We would appreciate Council’s preliminary feedback on that submission before further design work is undertaken.

**Discussion points**
- How has Council considered the submission to date?
- Which elements of the concept are considered to have strategic merit?
- Are there elements that Council considers problematic or in need of substantial revision?
- How does Council see the submission being used during preparation of the Dunoon Master Plan?
- Does Council consider the broad **ecovillage / clustered housing / shared infrastructure** concept appropriate for further investigation in Dunoon?
- What additional information would Council like the landowner to provide at the next stage?

**Requested outcome:**  
Clear preliminary feedback on the existing submission and guidance on how it should evolve during the Master Plan process.

---

## 6. Residential Density, Yield and Housing Typology

We would like to understand the assumptions behind the indicative housing yield for Dunoon and the degree of flexibility available through the Master Plan.

**Discussion points**
- Is the current **434-dwelling figure** intended to be:
  - a fixed cap;
  - a strategic estimate; or
  - an indicative yield to be refined through the Master Plan?
- What average densities and housing typologies is Council currently considering for Dunoon?
- Does Council expect broadly uniform density across the growth area, or will density vary by precinct according to:
  - servicing capacity;
  - topography;
  - environmental constraints;
  - proximity to the village centre;
  - road access; and
  - housing type?
- Would Council consider a **higher site-specific yield** where clustered housing, shared wastewater systems and larger areas of common open space produce better planning and infrastructure outcomes?
- What evidence would Council require to support a materially higher residential yield on our site?

**Requested outcome:**  
An understanding of Council’s density methodology and the technical evidence required to test a higher-yield ecovillage outcome.

---

## 7. Wastewater, Water, Roads and Other Infrastructure

Infrastructure capacity will be central to both the Master Plan and achievable residential yield.

**Discussion points**
- What servicing model is Council currently considering for wastewater in Dunoon?
- Is Council open in principle to:
  - precinct-scale communal wastewater systems;
  - a private centralised wastewater system; or
  - other decentralised servicing models?
- What level of wastewater investigation should landowners undertake during the Master Plan stage?
- Are there known constraints relating to:
  - potable water supply;
  - road capacity;
  - Dunoon Road / Duncan Road access;
  - stormwater;
  - electricity;
  - telecommunications; or
  - emergency services?
- What external infrastructure upgrades does Council presently expect may be required?
- What developer contribution, VPA, open-space dedication or infrastructure funding mechanisms are likely to apply?

**Requested outcome:**  
An early understanding of the major infrastructure constraints and what technical work should be commissioned now.

---

## 8. Environmental and Technical Investigation Requirements

We would like to avoid duplicating work and ensure that any technical studies commissioned by the landowner align with Council’s requirements.

**Discussion points**
- What studies does Council consider necessary before or during the Master Plan, including:
  - ecology / biodiversity;
  - bushfire;
  - contamination;
  - old cattle dip investigation;
  - agricultural land / land-use conflict;
  - Aboriginal heritage;
  - traffic;
  - stormwater;
  - wastewater;
  - water servicing;
  - geotechnical; and
  - flooding?
- Which studies should be commenced immediately and which should wait until the Master Plan is more advanced?
- Does Council have preferred scopes, standards or consultant requirements for these investigations?

**Requested outcome:**  
A preliminary technical study checklist and advice on study sequencing.

---

## 9. Commercial, Employment and Community Uses

The original Dirahny Ecovillage concept included a **Village Hub** and **Work Precinct**. We would like to understand whether Council sees scope for these or other complementary uses within the future growth area.

**Discussion points**
- Does Council support the principle of including small-scale commercial, employment or community uses within the future Dunoon growth area?
- Could the Master Plan consider uses such as:
  - neighbourhood retail / café;
  - childcare;
  - health or allied-health services;
  - flexible workspaces;
  - local trade / storage uses;
  - community facilities;
  - food production / farmers market; or
  - other village-supporting uses?
- Would Council prefer these uses to be concentrated within an identified village centre or distributed through the growth area?
- What strategic or market evidence would Council require before allocating land for non-residential uses?
- Are there particular uses Council considers desirable or inappropriate for Dunoon?

**Requested outcome:**  
Guidance on the role, scale and location of non-residential uses that may appropriately form part of the Master Plan.

---

## 10. Pathway After the Master Plan

We would like Council to confirm the expected statutory planning pathway once the Master Plan has progressed sufficiently.

**Discussion points**
- At what stage would Council accept a landowner-led **Planning Proposal**?
- What does Council mean by the Master Plan being “significantly prepared” for this purpose?
- What is the anticipated pathway from:
  1. Master Plan;
  2. Planning Proposal;
  3. Gateway Determination;
  4. LEP amendment / rezoning;
  5. Development Control Plan;
  6. subdivision / ecovillage DA?
- Which parts of the Planning Proposal and DA technical work can be prepared in parallel with the latter stages of the Master Plan?
- What are Council’s current indicative timeframes for each statutory stage?
- Would Council expect a precinct-wide planning proposal or individual landowner planning proposals?

**Requested outcome:**  
A clear agreed planning pathway from Master Plan through rezoning and DA, with an understanding of where parallel work is possible.

---

## 11. Agreed Next Steps

At the end of the meeting, we would like to confirm:

1. Council’s current working timetable for **State endorsement**.
2. The status and intended programme for the **Dunoon Master Plan**.
3. The mechanism for **ongoing landowner engagement and updates**.
4. Council’s preliminary feedback on the **June 2026 Dirahny Ecovillage submission**.
5. Council’s current approach to **density, housing typology and the 434-dwelling estimate**.
6. The key **infrastructure and technical investigations** required from the landowner.
7. The potential role of **commercial, employment and community uses**.
8. The statutory pathway and indicative timing from **Master Plan → Planning Proposal → Gateway → Rezoning → DA**.
9. Council’s nominated contact for ongoing coordination.
10. A suitable timing for the next technical / landowner workshop.
