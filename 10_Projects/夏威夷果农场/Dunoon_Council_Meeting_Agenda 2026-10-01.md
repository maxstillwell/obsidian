# Proposed Meeting Agenda

## Dunoon Village Growth Area / Dirahny Ecovillage  
**Meeting with Lismore City Council**

**Date:** Thursday, 1 October 2026  
**Time:** 11:00 am  
**Property:** 1306 Dunoon Road, Dunoon

### Purpose

To discuss Council’s programme and priorities for the Dunoon Village Growth Area, obtain preliminary feedback on the June 2026 Dirahny Ecovillage submission, and clarify landowner participation and the subsequent planning pathway.

### Project Context

- Total landholding: **142.49 ha**
- Area identified within the Dunoon Village Growth Area: **101.69 ha**
- Dirahny Ecovillage submission lodged in **June 2026**

---

## Agenda

### 1. Strategic Planning Status and Timing
- Current status and anticipated timing of NSW Government endorsement
- Whether the Dunoon Master Plan has commenced or is included in Council’s current or forthcoming work programme
- Council’s indicative timetable for preparation, consultation, exhibition and adoption of the Master Plan

### 2. Dunoon’s Role in Lismore’s Growth Strategy
- Council’s rationale for identifying Dunoon as a Village Growth Area
- Dunoon’s relative priority within Lismore’s broader housing strategy
- The basis and intended delivery horizon for the indicative **434-dwelling** yield

### 3. Dirahny Ecovillage Submission and Development Principles
- Council’s preliminary feedback on the June 2026 submission
- Council’s current thinking on residential density, housing diversity and site-specific yield
- The potential role of clustered housing, shared wastewater infrastructure, and appropriate commercial or community uses
- Further information or studies Council would like the landowner to prepare

### 4. Landowner Participation
- How major landowners will be involved during preparation of the Master Plan
- Opportunities to provide technical information and concept planning input
- Arrangements for regular updates and a nominated Council contact

### 5. Planning Pathway and Next Steps
- Expected pathway from **Master Plan → Planning Proposal → Gateway → Rezoning / DCP → Development Application**
- The stage at which landowner-led planning work may commence
- Work that may be undertaken in parallel
- Agreed actions, responsibilities and timing of the next meeting

---

### Documents Available for Discussion

- June 2026 **Dirahny Ecovillage Submission**
- Dunoon Village Growth Area and property mapping
- Current title, cadastral and available site information
